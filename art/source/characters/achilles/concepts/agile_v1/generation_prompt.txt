Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a NEW high-resolution character design master for ACHILLE, the playable hero of Catabase, an illustrated Greek underworld tactical RPG.

Primary request: invent an appealing original adult Achilles with an elegant, agile silhouette and economical graphic shapes, beautiful both enlarged on a character-selection screen and reduced to a small figure on a hand-painted isometric map. He is a swift, proud, clever young adult warrior, approximately 25–30, with presence and a distinctive face. This is a new design from this brief, not an edit or reproduction of an earlier character.

Single full-body character only. Portrait 2:3 composition at high native resolution. One large drawing, not a spritesheet or character board. Fixed elevated orthographic three-quarter view, about 25 degrees above eye-level, facing diagonally towards screen lower-right. Quiet alert contrapposto with believable planted feet, knees relaxed and feet separated, open space between elbows and waist, equipment carried beside the body. Entire spear, shield, crest, fingers and soles visible with generous margins. Character body occupies approximately 80 percent of the canvas height; avoid wasting resolution on background.

SHAPE DESIGN: deliberately stylized adult proportions, about 6.3 heads tall excluding the crest. Slightly emphasized expressive head, visible graceful neck, shoulders only moderately wider than the hips, narrow tapered ribcage and waist, long slender articulated legs, tapered forearms and practical readable hands and feet. Strength comes from posture, balance and silhouette. Arms and legs are smooth simplified volumes with a few painted planes; avoid delineated biceps, swollen deltoids, six-pack anatomy, bulky thighs, huge fists, huge boots, broad barrel chest or squat body. Adult, assured and charming, never a child or chibi and never a realistic bodybuilding illustration.

IDENTITY: warm ochre skin, fine angular clean-shaven adult face, distinct long straight nose, confident expressive brow and alert eyes, short sandy-blond hair grouped into three or four clear locks. Elegant open bronze headpiece framing the temples while leaving the face very readable. A SINGLE swept-back burnt-sienna crest with a striking slender flame/feather silhouette, compact and made of broad graphic shapes. No full face mask.
A light ivory linen cuirass made of simple smooth overlapping planes with restrained matte bronze edging, no sculpted muscular breastplate. A dark ink-teal short tunic, fitted waist, three long clean asymmetrical skirt panels ending above the knees with visible gaps allowing leg motion. One short burnt-sienna shoulder cloth draped behind one shoulder, ending at the hip and leaving the arms and legs visible. Restrained bronze bracer accents, slim open-knee greaves and fitted dark leather sandal-boots with simple clear soles. Only one simple Greek motif on the cloth, no ornament everywhere.
Anatomical RIGHT hand holds one slender straight ash-wood spear, a single elegant ivory-metal leaf blade at the TOP only, bronze socket, overall spear length about 1.1 body heights, carried almost upright. Anatomical LEFT forearm carries one modest round-to-oval deep teal shield, size approximately 27 percent of body height, thin matte bronze rim and a single clean ivory angled lightning/stride motif. The shield sits low and a little away from the torso, showing the waist and both legs. Hands genuinely grip the equipment; never swap or duplicate weapons.

ART: confident hand-drawn 2D painted game character, nuanced but economical color blocking, warm cream lit planes, cool blue-green shadow planes, selectively firm dark umber/ink-teal outlines and quieter internal contours. Two to three large value planes per material. Subtle visible paint within planes at large size, with clean silhouettes at small size. Soft warm upper-left illumination, matte bronze accents. Palette belongs naturally among honey limestone, muted olive vegetation, turquoise water and dark blue-green stone interiors, while ivory upper torso, warm face and burnt-sienna crest make the hero easy to find. A crafted original illustrated game design, not a 3D/PBR render, not glossy mobile-game armor, not generic superhero fantasy.

Scene/backdrop: genuine transparent RGBA background. No ground tile, no floor, no cast shadow, no environment, no glow, no particles, no text, no labels, no logo, no watermark and no checkerboard pattern. Only the single complete character cutout. Prioritize grace, recognizable silhouette, facial personality and restrained anatomy.
