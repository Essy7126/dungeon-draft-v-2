## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game sprite for Catabase, a hand-painted Greek underworld tactical RPG. Create ONE small circular stone pedestal for an animated hero selection screen, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. No character, no text, no background, no props. Hand drawn animation cel / painterly 2.5D style, strong clean dark outlines, broad simplified shapes, restrained painted texture. Shallow circular plinth made of dark desaturated sage and teal stone, narrow aged warm bronze rim, subtle Greek geometric engraving. Viewed from the front and slightly above, as a WIDE LOW ellipse, about 4:1 width to total height, top surface visible with enough flat space for a hero to stand. Soft warm illumination from upper left and cool turquoise side reflection. Center the pedestal in a wide 3:1 canvas with very little empty margin, keeping the complete outline intact. Gentle translucent contact shadow directly beneath, actual alpha everywhere else. Should blend into a dark emerald Greek sanctuary; no bright glowing border, no photorealistic texture, no shiny plastic. Deliver transparent PNG.
