# Première escale — prompt de la proposition v1

Mode : outil intégré image_gen, une image. Statut : proposition non intégrée.
Références : sanctuary.png (Autel), hall.png (Étal), land.png (barque du Seuil).

Create ONE image, the FIRST modest room of a five-room boat journey on the Lethe in Catabase. This is an art-direction proposal, not the whole series.
INPUT 1 (Autel des serments) and INPUT 2 (Etal du passeur) are the STRICT VISUAL STYLE REFERENCES. Match their broad softly painted shapes, LARGE simple slabs, restrained detail density, muted blue-green masonry, patinated bronze, amber localized lamplight, illustrative outlines, and close three-quarter game camera. It should look painted by the SAME artist as these two images. INPUT 3 is ONLY the boat continuity reference: reproduce the small brown wooden boat with its curved raised prow, three benches, rope and hanging amber lantern; do NOT borrow its gravel-textured arena or craggy cave aesthetic.
Scene: 'Les traces du Léthé', a quiet first stop just after leaving the Seuil by boat. A small low stone landing inside a sheltered river alcove. The turquoise water is the route: visibly enters at lower left and continues out at upper right, a SINGLE continuous navigable channel. The SAME boat is clearly visible, moored beside the lower edge of the landing, entirely in the water. A short simple landing step allows disembarking. Onshore a modest irregular paved landing, about 8-10 large slabs across, with enough open floor to stand and fight, framed by low old masonry and two roots at the back, two simple mooring posts. ONE small warm wall lamp. Everyday worn Greek-underworld construction, sparse and intimate. The water occupies a substantial part of the scene, not a tiny border around a huge arena. No connecting dry footpath to another room: travel onwards is by boat.
No monumental temple, no giant waterfall, no abyss, no exaggerated stalactites, no profusion of rubble or pebbles, no dramatic spectacle, no skulls, no lava, no altar, no merchant, no oversized gears. No characters, text, grid, HUD or labels. Preserve the gentle legibility and painted quality of the FIRST TWO references above everything else. Wide 16:10 composition, single finished painted scene.
